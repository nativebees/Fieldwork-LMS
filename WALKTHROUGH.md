# 90-second walkthrough

A script for an interview demonstration. The spoken lines total about 250 words, which is a brisk but workable pace for 90 seconds.

## Before you start

Stage the demo so the reveal happens live and nothing else needs typing.

1. Open the app and choose Reset, then Reset everything.
2. On the Account page, press Accept on the Marketing recommendation.
3. Go to Rehearsal. Choose the opening that begins "Here is what the snapshot shows" and press Say this. Stop there. Your progress is saved.
4. Return to the Account page. Use a window about 1440 pixels wide.

## If there is time for one question

Open the studio and press "Preview all" on the remaining suggestions. The dialog shows the assessment moving from `workflow-sequence@1` to `workflow-sequence@2`, with the criteria added and removed. That is the clearest view of how a change in the customer's brief becomes a change in what is assessed.

## What not to say

Do not describe this as live Claude coaching, as tested with users or as evidence of any business result. `EVIDENCE.md` lists what has and has not been shown.
